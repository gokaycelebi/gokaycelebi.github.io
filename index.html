How does pay-as-you-go work?

With pay-as-you-go, you only pay for the data you use. Add credit to your balance, and data is charged at the rate of the country you're in.

Good to know: rates are different in each destination. Check the rate for your next trip in the app before you fly.

When pay-as-you-go makes sense

  • You're visiting several countries on one trip
  • You mostly use maps and messaging, not video
  • You don't know yet how much data you'll need

If you'd rather know your costs upfront, a data plan may suit you better. Compare pay-as-you-go and data plans
